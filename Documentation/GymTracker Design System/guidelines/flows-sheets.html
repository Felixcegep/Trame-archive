• One sheet per job.Rescheduling uses a single shared sheet everywhere (Today, Calendar, notifications) — always offering "Shift remaining workouts" when a plan run exists. Never fork a lighter copy of a flow.
  • One effort metric per exercise.RPE or RIR is the primary target; the other renders read-only/derived with one explainer line. Never two editable effort fields on one sheet.
  • Conflicts protect data.When a workout is already active, Resume is the filled primary; "Start new" is secondary and states its consequence in one line. The safe choice always leads.
  • Consent before destruction.Restore/import states what happens to existing data ("This replaces your current data"); Restore carries destructive weight; partial saves confirm with exact counts (the 09b pattern).
  • Disabled buttons say why."Add" never sits gray and silent — inline caption ("Name required") or live count ("Add (2)").
  • Containers ≠ items.Folder rows get a count badge and distinct icon treatment so they never read as siblings of the items they contain.
  • Sheets are opaque.Temporary-task sheets never let stale parent content bleed through their top edge.
